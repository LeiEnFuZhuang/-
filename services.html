Our Manufacturing Services

From concept to delivery, we provide end-to-end apparel production solutions tailored for fashion brands of all sizes. Choose the partnership model that fits your needs.

🎯

OEM — Sample-Based Production

You provide the sample or tech pack, we manufacture to your exact specifications. Ideal for brands with existing designs who need reliable production partners.

  • Submit physical sample or detailed tech pack
  • We source matching fabrics and trims
  • 7-10 days for counter sample production
  • Adjustments until PP sample approval
  • Bulk production with AQL 2.5 inspection
  • Custom branding (labels, tags, packaging)
🎨

ODM — Design & Development

You share your concept or reference images, our design team develops the product from scratch. Perfect for brands without in-house design resources.

  • Share inspiration images or concept brief
  • Our team creates sketches and tech packs
  • Fabric and trim sourcing recommendations
  • Prototype development and fitting
  • Pattern optimization for production
  • Full bulk production and delivery

OEM vs ODM — Which is Right for You?

Compare our two partnership models to find the best fit for your brand

Feature OEM ODM
Design Source You provide sample/tech pack We design from your concept
MOQ 100 pcs per color per style 100 pcs per color per style

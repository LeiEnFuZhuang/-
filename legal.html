Return & Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Shipping

We ship worldwide via DHL, FedEx, or as per customer request. Delivery time depends on destination and order volume. Express shipping typically takes 10-15 business days.

Sample Orders

Sample fees are charged upfront and are refundable upon bulk order confirmation. Sample production takes 7-10 business days.

Returns & Refunds

Customized/OEM orders are non-returnable once production begins. For quality issues with stock items or samples, please contact us within 7 days of receipt with photos. We will evaluate and offer replacement or refund accordingly.

Who We Are

LAIEN GARMENTS is a vertically integrated manufacturer based in Panyu District, Guangzhou. Unlike pure trading companies, we own and operate our own garment factory — giving you direct access to production, transparent pricing, and full quality control.

With over 10 years of industry experience, we handle the entire apparel supply chain in-house: from fabric sourcing and pattern making to cutting, sewing, finishing, and export logistics. This means faster turnaround, better quality consistency, and no middleman markup.

  • 7-10 days sampling turnaround

Why Guangzhou?

Guangzhou is one of the world's largest apparel manufacturing hubs, with a complete supply chain ecosystem spanning fabric markets, accessory suppliers, dyeing houses, and garment factories.

Being based in Panyu District — the heart of Guangzhou's garment industry — gives our factory direct access to:

  • Zhongda Fabric Market (largest in Asia)
  • Specialized accessory and trim suppliers
  • Experienced pattern makers and sample rooms
  • Efficient logistics to Guangzhou Port and airports

This proximity means faster sourcing, lower costs, and better quality control for your orders — all managed in-house from our factory.

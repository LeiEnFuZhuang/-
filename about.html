Who We Are

LAIEN GARMENTS is a Guangzhou-based women's apparel manufacturer specializing in OEM, ODM, and private label clothing production. We bridge the gap between emerging fashion brands and reliable manufacturing partners across China.

With over 10 years of industry experience, we understand the challenges brands face when sourcing apparel from overseas. That's why we've built a streamlined process that handles everything from design sampling to bulk production — so you can focus on growing your brand.

Why Guangzhou?

Guangzhou is one of the world's largest apparel manufacturing hubs, with a complete supply chain ecosystem spanning fabric markets, accessory suppliers, dyeing houses, and garment factories.

Being based in Panyu District — the heart of Guangzhou's garment industry — gives us direct access to:

  • Zhongda Fabric Market (largest in Asia)
  • Specialized accessory and trim suppliers
  • Experienced pattern makers and sample rooms
  • Efficient logistics to Guangzhou Port and airports

This proximity means faster sourcing, lower costs, and better quality control for your orders.
